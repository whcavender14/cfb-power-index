# Derived metrics used by the site

None of these change CFPi+ methodology. Each one is computed in `R/publish/export_site_data.R` from outputs the pipeline already produces. The browser only displays them.

| Metric | Definition | Inputs | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Projected margin** (`spread_home`) | `P_home − P_away + HFA` (HFA = 0 at a neutral site) | `sim$team_power` (FBS ratings plus the model's own non-FBS ratings) and `sim$simulation_assumptions$hfa` | The model's documented margin formula: the mean of the simulation's draw for that game. Exported for scheduled games only. |
| **Win probability** (`win_prob_home`) | `Φ(spread_home / σ)`, σ = `sim$simulation_assumptions$resid_sd` (15.650 for C2) | same | The exact probability that the simulation's draw `Normal(spread, σ)` is ≥ 0. The simulation's rounding keeps the continuous draw's sign, so this equals the simulated win rate in expectation. |
| **Simulated win rate** (`sim_home_win`) | Share of the 1,000 simulations the home team won | `sim$game_summary$home_percentage` | Exported as a cross-check. Differs from `win_prob_home` only by Monte Carlo noise (s.e. ≈ 1.6 pts at 50%). Not displayed. |
| **Matchup quality** (`quality`, 0–100) | `round(100 × S × C)`. S = mean of the two teams' strength, where strength = `1 − (rank − 1)/(N − 1)` among the N rated FBS teams (non-FBS = 0). C = closeness = `1 − |2p − 1|`, p = `win_prob_home` | ranks, `win_prob_home` | 100 would be the top two teams in a pure toss-up. A top-5 pair at 70/30 scores ≈ 58. An FCS mismatch scores ≈ 0. Homepage "Top games" = the week's highest scores. |
| **Rank** / **national off / def rank** | Order by power (ties by team id). Offense rank by `off_rating` desc; defense rank by `def_rating` asc (lower is better) | production snapshot | The same ordering `01_build_ratings.R` writes. |
| **Rank change / rating change** | Current minus the previous point in `history.json` (CFPi+ only: a published same-model archive, else the labelled reconstruction) | `history.json` | One source for every movement arrow, the What-changed page and the rankings PNG. For week 3 of 2026 the comparison is the reconstructed week 2 (`meta.movement_source`). Never compared against another model's ratings. |
| **Schedule strength (SOS)** | Mean current CFPi+ rating of the opponents: `sos_played` (final games), `sos_all` (full regular season), `sos_remaining` | `sim$team_power` (FBS and the model's non-FBS ratings) | No home-field adjustment. Higher = harder. |
| **Strength of record (SOR)** | `Σ (win − P_bench)` over final games, `P_bench = Φ((B − opp_power + HFA·loc)/σ)`, loc = +1 home, −1 away, 0 neutral | `sim$simulation_assumptions$cfp_ranking$benchmark_rating` (B = rating of the No. 60 team, `PRODUCTION$cfp_rank_benchmark`), HFA, σ | Exactly the "wins above benchmark" term the simulation's CFP ranking uses (`R/simulation/cfb_dynamic_playoffs.R`), evaluated on real results so far. |
| **Résumé rank** | Order by SOR desc, then losses asc, then `sos_played` desc, then team id | resume | Approved method: `RESUME_PROPOSAL.md`. Teams without a final game are unranked. |
| **What-if odds** | For the picks, keep the simulations in which every picked team won its game; each figure = count in those simulations / number kept. Expected record = mean wins – (games − mean wins) | `scenario.json` | No re-simulation and no rating change. With 1,000 simulations: warning below 100 matching seasons (a 50% figure is ±10 points at 95%); below 25, counts are shown instead of percentages; 0 = "never happened". Changes are shown in percentage points against the published odds. |
| **Best win / worst loss** | Final win against the highest-rated opponent / final loss to the lowest-rated opponent, by current rating | schedule, ratings | Ties: first found. |
| **Final-record distribution** | Raw count of simulations ending with each regular-season W–L | `sim$standings` | Counts sum to 1,000 and their mean equals projected wins (both enforced). The team-page chart groups a losing tail of records each under 1% into "W–L or worse" only when two or more records qualify; the table always lists every record with its raw count. |
| **Weekly explanation** | For each game whose result entered the ratings since the comparison week: result, opponent's comparison-week rank, and the result versus `P_team − P_opp + HFA·loc` from the comparison week's ratings (FBS and non-FBS) | `history.json` sources, schedule | Fixed sentence template built in R; no LLM anywhere. It states the result against the projection and does not claim the game alone caused the change (every rating is refit on all games). No game in the window: offense/defense deltas only. |
| **Conference aggregates** | Mean and median member power; members ranked in the top 25; `exp_playoff = Σ p_playoff`; mean member `sos_all`; non-conference record = final games not flagged `conference_game` | ratings, simulation, schedule | Membership from team metadata. Independents are one group, excluded from the conference ranking and title columns. |
| **Record** | W–L of final games with kickoff before the cutoff (the games the simulation treats as known), including FCS games. Conference record uses the schedule's `conference_game` flag | `sim$schedule` | Same games the simulation treats as known. A game that kicked off in the last 24 h before the cutoff counts here but is not yet in the ratings (`in_ratings = false`): 11 such games in week 3 of 2026. |
| **Per-team simulation shares** | Means over the 1,000 simulated seasons of: in field (`!is.na(seed)`), seed ≤ 4 (bye), seed 5–8 (host), `exit ≥ 2/3/4` (QF / semifinal / final), `exit == 5` (champion), `conf_champ`, `wins`; `seed_dist` = share per seed | `sim$standings` | The exporter checks that playoff, title, conference and wins match cfbseedR's own `sim$overall` to 1e-9. Exit codes come from `cfbseedR:::sims_simulate_playoffs`: 1 = lost first round … 5 = champion. |
| **Bid type** (`p_auto`, `p_at_large`) | Auto = in the field **and** (a P4 conference champion, **or** the best-ranked team (by the simulation's `cfp_rank`) from the American, C-USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 or Sun Belt, **or** Notre Dame ranked ≤ 12). Otherwise at-large | `sim$standings` | Replicates `cfbseedR::cfb_playoff_seeds(autobid = "2026")`. Checked in all 1,000 sims (Stage 2 report). |
| **Projected field** (`representative_field`) | The simulated season maximizing `Σ log P(team gets its seed)` over its 12 seeded teams, where P comes from `seed_dist` | `sim$standings` | A real simulated outcome, so it obeys the selection rules. Not a forecast that every seed holds. `sims_with_identical_field` says how often that exact seeding occurred. |
| **Rating history** | Per week: power, off, def and rank among FBS (by power, ties by team id) | `history.json` | See `DATA_CONTRACT_V2.md` for sources and the reconstruction guard. |
| **Probability display** | Exact 0 or 1 out of 1,000 simulations shows as "<0.1%" / ">99.9%" | | Avoids implying certainty. Independents show "—" for conference title. |
